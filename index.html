Protecting Enterprise IP: Why Zero-Upload Processing Is Essential for Sensitive Design Files

The Overlooked Attack Vector: Third-Party Online File Converters

When enterprise security leaders audit data leakage vectors, they typically focus on endpoint protection, phishing prevention, and encrypted storage. Yet, an alarming blind spot frequently persists across creative, marketing, and product divisions: unvetted third-party web converters.

When an internal stakeholder needs to view an unreleased hardware schematic, embargoed brand identity, or legal packaging artwork saved in PSD format, they often Google "free online PSD viewer" and blindly upload the file. By doing so, they transmit invaluable corporate intellectual property across the open internet to unknown cloud servers with dubious logging and retention policies.

  • Frictionless Enterprise Compliance: Satisfies strict corporate compliance frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR) because confidential data remains strictly on the client device.
